Not you being dumb, that's how 0.2.6 works, and it's the wrong design. Each press of Texel Paint, or the D key with the cursor over the canvas (quicker than the button), starts one stroke: it follows your cursor and ends when you click or let go of the left mouse button, and the next stroke needs another press. Nothing is bound to the mouse button alone because left-click is Blender's select. A proper paint mode, where you switch painting on once and draw until you press Esc, is queued as the next fix after 0.2.7. Thanks for sticking with it, and for the kind words about the demo.
