Texel · who this is for

Five ways this audience makes characters — and where Texel belongs.

Every figure below was modelled and textured with Texel's own raster core, under identical lighting and one camera. Ranking is itch.io tag counts pulled 2026‑09‑09, not taste.
Most common

PS1 / PSX retro

6,407 games · 2,398 asset packs
Lethal Company, Signalis, Crow Country, Paratopic
500–3,000 tris. One 64–128px atlas, nearest filtering, flat shading. The look is finished at render time: affine texture warp, vertex jitter, dithering, 320×240 output.
Texel fits — best

Needs it most. The warp shader changes how the texture reads on the model, so you cannot paint it blind in Aseprite and guess.

2nd

Voxel

3,068 games · 967 asset packs
Bonfire Peaks, Cube World, Trove · 563 itch games tagged made‑with‑MagicaVoxel
A grid of cubes, typically 64³. Colour is assigned per cube in MagicaVoxel or Blockbench and baked to vertex colours or a palette strip on export.
Not Texel's job

There is no UV texture to paint. Saying otherwise would be selling you something the tool does not do.

3rd

Billboard / 2.5D

594 games (fps + pixel‑art)
Nightmare Reaper, Hedon · the HD‑2D family (Octopath Traveler) above it
Not geometry at all. A camera‑facing plane wearing a hand‑drawn sheet, 32–64px a frame, with pre‑drawn angles swapped as the camera orbits.
Half — sheet only

Texel can paint the sprite sheet in the Image Editor, but there is no model to paint on. This is the one style where a flat card is the right answer.

4th

Clean pixel low‑poly

— not separable from PSX on itch
Anodyne 2: Return to Dust — low-poly overworld, 2D pixel interiors
The same mesh and the same atlas as PSX, minus the corruption. No warp, no jitter, no dither — crisp and stable, so shading can carry more of the work.
Texel fits — best

Identical mechanic: a UV-mapped low-poly mesh wearing a small painted texture. Exactly what the tool is.

5th

N64 retro

182 games
Corn Kidz 64
N64 hardware had perspective-correct mapping and subpixel precision, so no warp and no jitter. The signature is Gouraud shading and a bilinear-filtered low-res texture — soft, not blocky. Segmented rigid parts.
Texel fits

Same mechanic as PSX and clean low-poly, on a smaller population.

Worth knowing: itch's plain low-poly tag returns 26,715 games — but the packs that dominate it (Synty POLYGON, KayKit) carry no pixel texture at all. That is a different, larger audience Texel has nothing to sell to.
